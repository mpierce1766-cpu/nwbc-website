What to Expect

When You Arrive

We are a close-knit, loving church family where you are never just a face in the crowd. From the moment you walk through our doors, you will be welcomed with a warm smile and a handshake. Because we are a smaller congregation, you will find genuine Christian fellowship where people truly care about getting to know you and your family.
