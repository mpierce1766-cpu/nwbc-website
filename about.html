Our Story

Family, Friends, and Faith

The three most vital relationships in a person’s life.

  • Family
  • Friends
  • Faith

I’m Pastor Bob Gray and we at Northwest Baptist Church of San Antonio, Texas value these relationships. We want to encourage you to join with us as we seek to establish these fundamental relationships within our church, our neighborhoods, and our community. When these relationships and our lives are built on the truth of God’s Word, people are better able to withstand the storms of life.

Our church focuses on developing a heart for God as we proclaim the truths that will transform our lives and relationships with family, friends, and loved ones.

There are two places where a person should feel wanted, loved, and cared for – their home and their church. At Northwest Baptist Church of San Antonio, we strive to strengthen the home and church families.

A home like this provides a rich, nurturing atmosphere for Family, Friends, and Faith to flourish. Northwest Baptist Church is just such a place.

We are a group of Bible-believing Christians striving to maintain a balanced, Spirit-filled life in today’s times. San Antonio is a great place to raise our families, but it can become even greater as we build our lives on the principles of God’s Word through a personal relationship with the Lord Jesus Christ.

We invite you to come, see, and be a part of a church desiring to strengthen your faith, build strong marriages, raise godly children, develop new and meaningful friendships, and help others do the same. We believe you will find a warm welcome and good reason to make our church your spiritual home.

— Pastor Bob Gray
