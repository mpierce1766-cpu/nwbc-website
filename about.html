Our Story

Family, Friends, and Faith

The three most vital relationships in a person’s life.

  • Family
  • Friends
  • Faith

I’m Pastor Bob Gray and we at Northwest Baptist Church in the San Antonio area value these relationships. We want to encourage you to join with us as we seek to establish these fundamental relationships within our church, our neighborhoods, and our community. When these relationships and our lives are built on the truth of God’s Word, people are better able to withstand the storms of life.

Our church focuses on developing a heart for God as we proclaim the truths that will transform our lives and relationships with family, friends, and loved ones.

There are two places where a person should feel wanted, loved, and cared for – their home and their church. At Northwest Baptist Church, we strive to strengthen the home and church families.

A home like this provides a rich, nurturing atmosphere for Family, Friends, and Faith to flourish. Northwest Baptist Church is just such a place.

We are a group of Bible-believing Christians striving to maintain a balanced, Spirit-filled life in today’s times. The San Antonio area is a great place to raise our families, but it can become even greater as we build our lives on the principles of God’s Word through a personal relationship with the Lord Jesus Christ.

We invite you to come, see, and be a part of a church desiring to strengthen your faith, build strong marriages, raise godly children, develop new and meaningful friendships, and help others do the same. We believe you will find a warm welcome and good reason to make our church your spiritual home.

— Pastor Bob Gray

Our Mission

Our mission is to glorify God by standing uncompromisingly on the preserved King James Bible, preaching the saving Gospel of Jesus Christ to the lost, and discipling born-again believers to live faithfully for His kingdom.

What We Believe

The Bible

We believe the Holy Scriptures are the verbally inspired, inerrant words of God, providentially preserved for the English-speaking world in the King James Bible. Translated from the preserved Masoretic Hebrew and Greek Textus Receptus, the King James Bible is our final, absolute authority in all matters of faith and practice.

  • 2 Timothy 3:16 All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness:
  • 2 Peter 1:20–21 Knowing this first, that no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation. For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.
  • Matthew 24:35 Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.

God

We believe in the one living, true, and holy God, the sovereign Creator of heaven and earth. He eternally exists and manifests Himself in three co-equal persons—the Father, the Word (Son), and the Holy Ghost—and these three are one God.

  • 1 John 5:7 For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one.
  • John 10:30 I and my Father are one.
  • Genesis 1:1 / John 1:1–3 In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth… In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. The same was in the beginning with God. All things were made by him;

Jesus Christ

We believe that the Lord Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, who was virgin-born and God manifest in the flesh, possessing sinless humanity and absolute deity. He died for our sins, shed His precious atoning blood on Calvary, rose bodily from the grave on the third day, and is coming again.

  • 1 Timothy 3:16 And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory.
  • Matthew 1:23 Behold, a virgin shall be with child, and shall bring forth a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us.
  • 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures:
  • Acts 1:11 / Revelation 22:20 This same Jesus, which is taken up from you into heaven, shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go into heaven… He which testifieth these things saith, Surely I come quickly. Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus.

Salvation

We believe salvation is a free gift of God’s grace, received solely through personal faith in the finished cross-work and shed blood of the Lord Jesus Christ, completely apart from human works, baptism, or the deeds of the law. Every repentant sinner who trusts in Him is instantly born again, justified freely by His grace, and kept eternally secure in Christ.

  • Ephesians 2:8–9 For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.
  • Romans 3:23–24 For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God; Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus:
  • John 3:3, 7 Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God… Marvel not that I said unto thee, Ye must be born again.

The Church

We believe the local New Testament church is an autonomous assembly of born-again, baptized believers, purchased by Jesus Christ with His own blood. United under Christ as its sole Head, the church is called out to worship the Lord, observe the scriptural ordinances, and faithfully preach the uncompromised Gospel of grace to all the world.

  • Acts 20:28 Take heed therefore unto yourselves, and to all the flock, over the which the Holy Ghost hath made you overseers, to feed the church of God, which he hath purchased with his own blood.
  • Colossians 1:18 And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.
